# UCP ecosystem crawl, September 30, 2026

## Scope and result

Reviewed announcements from September 23–30, 2026, the official UCP GitHub organization, and Upsonic/awesome-ucp. Added two entries to this repository and UCPList: Shopify Checkout WebMCP and the UCP Lodging Booking draft capability. The new records distinguish live tooling from draft work and include primary sources.

## Included

- [Shopify Checkout WebMCP](https://shopify.dev/changelog/posts/webmcp-support-for-checkout), released September 28. Its [documentation](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/carts-and-checkout/checkout-webmcp) explicitly says it implements `dev.ucp.shopping.checkout` in browser-registered WebMCP tools. Shopify describes the tools as live, but no merchant checkout was independently exercised.
- [UCP Lodging Booking](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/discussions/864), announced September 25 after [PR 780](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/780) merged September 24. The [specification](https://ucp.dev/draft/specification/lodging/booking/) is explicitly draft and subject to change.

## Checked, not added

- The [Universal-Commerce-Protocol organization](https://github.com/orgs/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/repositories) created no repositories in the window. Recent official discussions included the lodging announcement but no other independently listable integration.
- [Upsonic/awesome-ucp](https://github.com/Upsonic/awesome-ucp) had no commits in the window; its existing entries were already represented or were background resources rather than new integrations.
- [Worldline Global Collect Payment Handler](https://worldline.com/en/home/top-navigation/media-relations/press-release/pr-2026_09_14_01) was announced September 14, outside this crawl window.
- [Auth0 Auth for UCP](https://s205.q4cdn.com/566291348/files/doc_events/2026/Sep/23/Okta-Oktane-2026-Announcements.pdf) appears in Okta's September Oktane PDF, but the document is indexed as published September 22. The exact product announcement time was not independently established within this scan window, so it was excluded.
- [Shopware Agentic Commerce plugin](https://github.com/shopware/agentic-commerce/releases/tag/1.3.0) released September 17, also outside the window; a Shopware plugin entry already exists.
- [Flipkart buying pilot](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/26/google-tests-buying-from-walmart-owned-flipkart-through-gemini-and-ai-mode-in-india/) was not treated as a new UCP implementation. The article discusses UCP separately, and Flipkart is already listed.

Sources are public; this note contains no credentials or private customer data.
